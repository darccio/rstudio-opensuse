# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Be considerate, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and respect
other people's time and privacy. Give constructive feedback and assume good
intent when asking for clarification.

Harassment, discriminatory or threatening language, personal attacks, and
publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. These
expectations apply to issues, reviews, and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to [cc@dario.cat](mailto:cc@dario.cat). The maintainer
will review reports, protect private information, and may remove content or
restrict participation when needed. This project takes inspiration from the
[openSUSE Guiding Principles](https://en.opensuse.org/openSUSE:Guiding_principles);
reports here are handled by this project's maintainer.
